What this project explores

Shared rides can save money, but extra pickups make arrival less predictable. This concept explores whether explicit time limits and a single co-rider make that trade-off easier to understand.

Dynamic matching already exists. This project explores rider control and predictability; it does not claim to invent ride-sharing.

Evidence, assumptions & next test

Motivated by a family incident and secondary research. Formal discovery interviews and prototype usability testing have not been completed. All fares, routes and timings are examples. The next step is observing 3-5 people use this prototype.

Added time is a prediction, not a guarantee. Same-destination constraints may reduce match availability. Traffic changes, driver compensation and emergency procedures remain open questions.

Advanced demo controls

Manual role selection, offer expiry and technical checks. These are outside the passenger experience.

Concept only. No real ride, payment, GPS or platform integration. No measured adoption or business impact.
